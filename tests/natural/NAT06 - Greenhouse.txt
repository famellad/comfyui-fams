The Off-World Greenhouse (Future)

Subject: A sleek, white-and-chrome humanoid robot with no face, but rather a visor covered with LED lights.

Time of Day: Mid-morning (bright, clean sunlight).

Place: A geodesic bio-dome greenhouse on Mars (crimson red desert landscape with reddish skies devoid of life visible through the glass panels).

Clothing Style: A simple orange canvas utility vest worn over its mechanical chassis.

Action: Using delicate, multi-jointed fingers to gently pinch the stem of a blooming red rose.
